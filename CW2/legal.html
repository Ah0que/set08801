Legal Stuff: The Rules of Our Website


Welcome to the rulebook for using our corner of the internet! Just like every great game has rules to keep things fair and fun, our website has some guidelines too. Please read them carefully before you start playing (or browsing!).

Rule 1: How to Play Nice (Terms of Use & Disclaimer)


This website, "Children's Games and Their Rules," is like a big book of game ideas! We share information about how to play games, but please remember:

We're sharing ideas, not giving professional advice.
The information is for fun and learning.
Playing games can sometimes involve running or moving, so please be safe and play in suitable areas! We can't be responsible for any bumps or scraped knees that happen while playing the games you read about here.
The rules we list are common ways to play, but many games have variations! Feel free to adapt them, but remember that's your own game version.
By using this website, you agree to play by these rules!

Rule 2: Sharing is Caring, But Give Credit (Copyright)


All the words, pictures (if any show up without CSS/JS!), and game rules you find written uniquely on this website belong to us. It's like our special collection of game treasures!

You can read and learn from them, but please don't copy and share them as your own on other websites or places without asking us first or giving us clear credit (like saying "Found on Children's Games and Their Rules").

Rule 3: Keeping Your Secrets Safe (Privacy)


We believe in playing fair, and that includes being honest about any information we might collect. Since this page has no fancy code (like CSS or JavaScript), we keep things very simple.

We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal details to just look at the game rules. We don't use cookies or tracking things that follow you around the internet.

If you contact us (like sending an email if we provide a contact address elsewhere), we will only use your information to reply to you. We won't share your contact information with others.

Basically, we respect your privacy like the best hiding spot in hide-and-seek!

Rule 4: Exploring Other Playgrounds (External Links)


Sometimes, we might link to other websites that have more cool game ideas or related information. Think of these links like paths leading to other playgrounds.

Once you click on a link and leave our website, we can't be responsible for the rules or what happens on that other playground. Always be careful and read their rules (legal pages) too!

Rule 5: Sometimes Rules Change (Updates to this Page)


Just like game rules can evolve over time, we might need to update these website rules sometimes.

If we change anything important, we'll try to make a note of it. It's a good idea to check this page once in a while, just like you'd check if the rules of tag have changed since you last played!

Thanks for reading our website rules! Now go have fun playing!
