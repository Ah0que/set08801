Comprehensive Guide on How to Play Jenga

Introduction to Jenga

Jenga is a game of physical skill where players take turns removing blocks from a tower and stacking them on top. The goal is to be the last player to successfully stack a block without the tower falling over.

Setting Up the Game

Follow these steps to set up the Jenga tower:


Start with all 54 Jenga blocks.
Build the tower by stacking three blocks per layer, placed side by side along their longest dimension.
Alternate the direction of the blocks in each layer. For example, if the first layer's blocks run horizontally, the second layer's blocks should run vertically, and so on.
The starting tower will have 18 layers.
Ensure the tower is built on a flat and stable surface.

Gameplay - Taking a Turn


Players take turns in order. On your turn, you must:

Remove a single block from any level below the highest completed story. A completed story is a layer of three blocks that is full.
Use only one hand to touch the tower and remove the block. You can use the same hand to steady the tower while removing the block.
You may touch blocks to find a loose one, but if you move a block out of place while testing, you must either fix it (using only one hand) or commit to removing it. Once a block is moved from its original position, you must either remove it or return it to its original position before touching another block.
Once the block is successfully removed, place it on the top of the tower to complete the next layer.
Blocks on the top layer must be placed at right angles to the layer below them. Complete a three-block layer before starting a new one.
Your turn ends when you have successfully placed your block on top and the next player is ready, or when the tower falls.

Winning and Losing


The game ends when the tower collapses.
The player whose turn it was when the tower falls is the loser. This includes if the tower falls while they are removing or placing a block, or any time before the next player has successfully removed a block.
The player who completed their turn successfully just before the tower fell is the winner.

Tips for Playing


Take your time and be patient.
Carefully observe the tower to find loose blocks.
Think about how removing or placing a block might affect the tower's stability for the next player.
Practice a steady hand!

Conclusion

Jenga is a simple game to learn but challenging to master. It's a great test of skill and nerves. Set up the tower and see who can last the longest!
