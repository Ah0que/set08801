Basic Guide on How to Play Hide and Seek

Introduction to Hide and Seek


Hide and Seek is a simple and fun game played by two or more players. One player is designated as the "seeker," and the others are "hiders." The seeker closes their eyes and counts to a predetermined number while the hiders find places to conceal themselves. The seeker then tries to find the hiders.

What You Need


At least two players.
An agreed-upon playing area with places to hide.
An agreed-upon spot to be the "counting spot" or "home base."

Setting Up the Game


Choose one player to be the first seeker.
All players agree on the boundaries of the playing area.
All players agree on the counting spot (e.g., a tree, a wall).
All players agree on the number the seeker will count to (e.g., 10, 20, 100).

How to Play


The seeker goes to the counting spot, closes their eyes (or covers them), and begins counting aloud to the agreed-upon number.
While the seeker is counting, the other players run and find places to hide within the agreed-upon boundaries.
Hiders must be fully concealed and quiet.
Once the seeker finishes counting, they shout a phrase like "Ready or not, here I come!" or "Coming!" and open their eyes.
The seeker leaves the counting spot and begins searching for the hiders.

Being Found


When the seeker sees a hider, they must race back to the counting spot and touch it, shouting the hider's name (e.g., "Tag, [Hider's Name]!").

If a hider can get out of their hiding spot and race back to the counting spot before the seeker tags them, they can touch the counting spot and shout a phrase like "Free!" or "Home Free!" If they do this, they are safe and cannot be the next seeker.

Winning and Becoming the Next Seeker


The first hider that the seeker finds AND successfully tags at the counting spot usually becomes the next seeker.
If a hider gets "Home Free" before being tagged, they are safe.
The game for that round ends when all hiders have been found and tagged, or when the last hider gets "Home Free."
If the last hider gets "Home Free," the original seeker usually remains the seeker for the next round.

Basic Rules Summary


One seeker counts, others hide.
Seeker searches after counting.
Seeker tags hiders at the counting spot.
Hiders can get "Home Free" by touching the counting spot before being tagged.
First tagged hider is usually the next seeker.

Conclusion


Hide and Seek is a simple game of hiding and finding. It's easy to learn and can be played almost anywhere with enough space and hiding spots. Have fun!
