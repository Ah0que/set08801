About Us - Children's Games and Their Rules

About Us: Unlocking the World of Play!


Welcome to Children's Games and Their Rules! We're not just a website; we're a secret clubhouse, a treasure map, and a gateway to endless adventures, all powered by the magic of play!

Our Story: Born from Giggles and Grins


Every grown-up remembers that one game — the one played until the sun went down, the one that sparked the loudest laughter, the one that turned a simple backyard into a fantastical realm. Our story began with a shared love for those timeless moments. We realised that while the world keeps spinning faster, the simple joy of a game of tag, a strategic round of hide-and-seek, or the quiet concentration of marbles is something truly precious.


But sometimes, memories fade, rules get fuzzy, and new generations need a little nudge to discover these wonders. That's where we come in! We decided to create a place where the spirit of childhood games lives on, a comprehensive guide for parents, teachers, guardians, and anyone who believes in the power of play.

What We Do: Guardians of the Game


Think of us as the friendly keepers of the game treasury! Our mission is simple:


Collect and Share: We gather the rules and variations of beloved children's games from around the corner and across the globe. From classic playground staples to rainy-day indoor fun, we're building a comprehensive library of play.
Clarify and Simplify: Game rules should be easy to understand, not complicated puzzles! We break down how to play each game with clear, simple instructions, so you can spend less time reading and more time playing.
Inspire New Adventures: We hope to spark your imagination! Discover new games, find variations on old favourites, and get inspired to create your own playful moments.
Celebrate Play: We believe play is fundamental to childhood development — fostering creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, and just plain fun! We're here to champion the importance of unstructured play in a busy world.

Who We Are: A Bunch of Big Kids!


We're a team of enthusiasts — parents, educators, game lovers, and maybe just a few perpetual big kids — who are passionate about preserving and promoting the joy of children's games. We pour our hearts (and maybe a few scraped knees from "testing" games) into making this website a valuable resource for you.


Join us in celebrating the timeless fun of children's games! Let's play!
