Basketball is a dynamic team sport played by two teams of five players each on a rectangular court. The objective is to shoot a ball through a hoop 10 feet (3.05 meters) high that is mounted to a backboard at each end of the court. The game is played in quarters, and the team with the most points at the end of the game wins.

Basic Rules of Basketball
Each team has 5 players on the court at a time.
Points are scored by shooting the ball through the opponent's hoop.
A regular shot inside the three-point line is worth 2 points.
A shot made from outside the three-point line is worth 3 points.
A free throw (awarded after certain fouls) is worth 1 point.
Players can only move with the ball by dribbling (bouncing the ball while walking or running) or passing.
Once a player stops dribbling, they cannot start dribbling again (double dribble).
A player cannot run with the ball without dribbling (traveling).
The ball must stay within the court boundaries.
Physical contact that is deemed unfair is called a foul.
There are time limits for certain actions, such as the shot clock (time to shoot) and closely guarded situations.
